# Question Day 005

## 1. What is Ethernet Switching?

Answer:
Ethernet switching is the process of forwarding Ethernet frames based on MAC addresses.

---

## 2. What is a MAC Address Table?

Answer:
A MAC Address Table stores MAC addresses and the interfaces where they were learned.

---

## 3. How does a switch learn MAC addresses?

Answer:
A switch learns MAC addresses by examining the source MAC address of incoming Ethernet frames.

---

## 4. What happens when the destination MAC address is unknown?

Answer:
The switch floods the frame out of all ports except the incoming port.

---

## 5. What is a Broadcast Frame?

Answer:
A broadcast frame is sent to every device in the broadcast domain.

---

## 6. What is a Unicast Frame?

Answer:
A unicast frame is sent to one specific destination device.

---

## 7. What is Frame Flooding?

Answer:
Flooding occurs when the destination MAC address is unknown to the switch.

---

## 8. Why is the MAC Address Table important?

Answer:
It allows the switch to forward frames efficiently to the correct destination.
