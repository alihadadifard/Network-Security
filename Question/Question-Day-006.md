# Question Day 006

## 1. What is an Ethernet Frame?

Answer:
An Ethernet Frame is the data unit used for communication on Ethernet networks.

---

## 2. What is the purpose of the Frame Check Sequence (FCS)?

Answer:
The FCS detects transmission errors in Ethernet frames.

---

## 3. What is CRC?

Answer:
CRC (Cyclic Redundancy Check) is the algorithm used to calculate the FCS.

---

## 4. What is the Broadcast MAC Address?

Answer:
FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF

---

## 5. What is MAC Address Aging?

Answer:
It is the process of removing inactive MAC addresses from the MAC address table after a period of inactivity.

---

## 6. What is the Ethernet Type field?

Answer:
It identifies the protocol encapsulated inside the Ethernet frame.

---

## 7. What is ARP?

Answer:
ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) maps IPv4 addresses to MAC addresses.
