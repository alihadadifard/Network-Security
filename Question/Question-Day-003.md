# Question Day 003

## 1. What is TCP/IP?

Answer:
TCP/IP is a suite of protocols used for network communication.

---

## 2. How many layers are in the TCP/IP Model?

Answer:
Five layers.

---

## 3. What is Encapsulation?

Answer:
Encapsulation is the process of adding headers and trailers as data moves down the network layers.

---

## 4. What is Decapsulation?

Answer:
Decapsulation is the process of removing headers and trailers as data moves up the layers.

---

## 5. What is a PDU?

Answer:
PDU stands for Protocol Data Unit.

---

## 6. What is Layer 3 of the OSI Model?

Answer:
Network Layer.

---

## 7. What is Layer 4 of the TCP/IP Model?

Answer:
Transport Layer.

---

## 8. Why do layered models exist?

Answer:
To organize network communication into separate responsibilities.
