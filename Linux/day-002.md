## Commands Learned

* echo

* mv
* rm
* clear

## Command Descriptions

### echo

Write text into a file.

Example:
echo "Hello" > notes.txt

---

### cp

Copy files.

Example:
cp notes.txt backup.txt

---

### mv

Move or rename files.

Example:
mv backup.txt final.txt

---

### rm

Remove files.

Example:
rm final.txt

---

### clear

Clear the terminal screen.

Example:
clear
